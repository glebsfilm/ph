— II. About the photographer

Hello.
Thanks for stopping by.

I got hooked on film photography after finding my parents' old camera and developing a forgotten roll inside.

I shoot 135 and 120 film on SLR and TLR cameras — the slow, deliberate kind of picture-taking that asks you to look first and shoot second. Every frame is metered, focused and composed by hand; every roll developed and scanned with care.

Feel free to drop me a line on Instagram or Lomography, or send an email to glebsfilm@gmail.com — questions, prints, collaborations, all welcome.
